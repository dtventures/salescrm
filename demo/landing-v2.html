How it works

Everything about a customer, in one place.

Emails, meetings, notes, link clicks and page visits land on the right person automatically. Nothing is retyped.

Read-only sync

Trellis reads your Gmail and calendar history once, then keeps up as new mail arrives. It never changes your inbox.

Matched, never guessed

People are matched by email address, tracked link, form or login. Anything less certain stays at company level.

Nothing sends itself

Drafts wait for you. Every send passes the same checks, and every action is written to an activity log.

FAQ

Good questions.

Will Trellis send emails without asking me?

No. It writes drafts and you approve every send. Automatic sending is off for everyone during the beta.

Does it replace my CRM?

Yes. Contacts, companies and deals are built in and fill themselves from your email, calendar and notes. It works alongside Gmail and Google Calendar rather than replacing them.

How does it know who visited my site?

Only when it can be sure. A prospect who clicks a tracked link in your email, fills a form or logs in is identified. Trellis never guesses who anonymous visitors are.

Do email opens count?

They’re shown but never counted toward a signal, because scanners and privacy proxies trigger them. Clicks, replies, forms and page visits do count.

How far back does it read my email?

You choose: three months, one year or two years. You can also skip colleagues, personal contacts, newsletters and receipts.

Is it for a solo founder or a team?

Both. The beta is for founder-led B2B teams of one to twenty sellers on Google Workspace.

What if I want out?

Disconnect Google in one click, take a full export, and ask us to delete everything.
