FAQ

Good questions.

Will Trellis send emails without asking me?

No. It writes drafts and you approve every send. Automatic sending is off for everyone during the beta.

Does it replace my CRM?

Yes. Contacts, companies and deals are built in and fill themselves from your email, calendar and notes. It works alongside Gmail and Google Calendar rather than replacing them.

How does it know who visited my site?

Only when it can be sure. A prospect who clicks a tracked link in your email, fills a form or logs in is identified. Trellis never guesses who anonymous visitors are.

Is it for a solo founder or a team?

Both. The beta is for founder-led B2B teams of one to twenty sellers on Google Workspace.

What if I want out?

Disconnect Google in one click, take a full export, and ask us to delete everything.
