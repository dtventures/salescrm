FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Trellis replace my CRM?

Yes. Contacts, companies, deals and your pipeline are built in, and they fill themselves from your email, calendar and notes. You can import existing records from a CSV.

Do I still use Gmail and Google Calendar?

Yes. Trellis connects to them rather than replacing them. Anything you send or schedule shows up in Trellis, and emails you send from Trellis go out from your own Gmail address.

How does website and email tracking work?

Add one snippet to your site and send emails with tracked links. When a prospect clicks, Trellis ties that browser to them and shows the pages they visit afterward. It never guesses who anonymous visitors are, and opens are shown but not trusted.

What happens to my meeting notes?

Paste them, upload them, or connect a supported notes tool. Trellis links each note to the meeting and deal and pulls out next steps, objections and promises.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Teams of up to 20 sellers share one workspace with member, manager and admin roles. Managers see the whole pipeline and deals at risk; admins control connections, rules and data retention.

Will Trellis ever send email without me?

No. Automatic sending is off for everyone during the beta. If it ever arrives, it will need an admin to turn it on and every safety check to pass.

Does it work with Outlook?

Not yet. The beta supports Google Workspace. You can export everything as CSV at any time.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Your data is used only to serve your workspace and is never used to train shared models.
