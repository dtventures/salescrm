p.06 · The list

p.08 · House rules

House rules

Written in the notebook before the first line of code. We don’t break these to ship faster.

  1. Events before fields.Raw history is never overwritten.
  2. Show your receipts.Every summary, score and answer links to its source.
  3. Know who, or do nothing.No action unless we’re sure who it’s for.
  4. You press send.Drafts first. Autonomy is a later, explicit setting.
  5. First-party only.Works without buying data about strangers.
  6. Your data is yours.Never used to train shared models. Export or delete anytime.
