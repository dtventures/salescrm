FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will Trellis ever send email without me?

No. Trellis writes drafts and you send them. Automatic sending is off for everyone during the beta, and if it ever arrives it will need an admin to turn it on and every safety check to pass.

What does Trellis read?

Your Gmail, Google Calendar, meeting notes you paste or upload, and activity on your own website if you add the snippet. You choose how far back to import, and you can skip colleagues, personal contacts and newsletters.

Do I have to install anything on my website?

It’s optional. The website snippet lets Trellis see when a prospect you emailed visits your pricing or security page. Everything else works without it.

Does it work with Outlook or HubSpot?

Not yet. The beta supports Google Workspace. You can export everything as CSV at any time.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Your data is used only to serve your workspace and is never used to train shared models.

Can I leave?

Anytime. Disconnect Google in one click, take a full export, and ask us to delete everything.
